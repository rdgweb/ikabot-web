Free trial

The 7 trial days have no charge and need no card. If you do not subscribe, the agents stop receiving jobs when the trial ends; nothing is charged.

Cancelling

Cancel whenever you want in My organization > Plan. The subscription stops renewing and the plan lasts until the end of the period already paid.

Refunds

  • First charge: if you ask within 7 days of paying, we refund the full amount (right of withdrawal, art. 49 of the Brazilian Consumer Code).
  • Renewals: a month already started is not refunded; after cancelling you keep access until the end of the paid period.
  • Wrong or duplicate charges: always refunded.

Ask through {% if legal.email %}{{ legal.email }}{% else %}the contact page{% endif %}. The refund goes back through the same payment method, via Asaas, within 10 business days — on cards, the statement date depends on the issuing bank.

Leaving and deleting data

After cancelling, you may ask us to delete your organization's data, as described in the privacy policy.
