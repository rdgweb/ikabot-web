2. What we keep

  • Your IkaHub account: username, email and password (stored only as a hash).
  • Game accounts: GameForge email and password (encrypted), worlds and what the automation reads from the accounts — cities, resources, troops, reports and in-game messages.
  • Agents and proxies: machine name, agent version, address and access data of the proxies you add (encrypted when they are passwords).
  • Activity log: who did what and when, with IP and browser, for 12 months.
  • Terms acceptance: version, date, IP and browser.
  • Plan: tier, amounts, payment dates and the Asaas ids. The payer's name, tax id and card data stay with Asaas, not with IkaHub.
  • Invite requests: email, name, number of accounts, message and IP.
  • Telegram: the id of the chat you link and the messages exchanged with the bot.
  • Site visits: page counts without cookies and without storing the IP (Umami, on our own server).

3. Why and on what legal basis

  • Running the service you signed up or asked to try (performance of a contract).
  • Security, fraud prevention and the activity log (legitimate interest and legal obligation).
  • Answering your invite request and support (steps taken at your request).
  • Understanding, in aggregate, which site pages are visited (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • Gameforge: the agent signs in to the game with your credentials, on your behalf.
  • Proxy providers you choose: the game traffic goes through them.
  • Asaas, which processes the plan's payments.
  • Telegram, if you link a chat (may involve international transfer).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

5. For how long

While your account exists. If you leave, we delete your organization's data; the activity log and the terms acceptance are kept up to 12 months for security and legal defence. Unanswered invite requests are deleted within 12 months.

6. Your rights

You may ask for confirmation of processing, access, correction, portability, anonymization or deletion of your data, and information about sharing (Brazilian LGPD, art. 18). {% if legal.privacy_email %}Write to {{ legal.privacy_email }}.{% else %}Use the contact page.{% endif %} You may also complain to the Brazilian data protection authority (ANPD).

7. Security

Encrypted game passwords, one key per agent stored only as a hash, agents connected over HTTPS only, each organization's data isolated, and an activity log.

8. Cookies

We only use the cookies needed to keep you signed in and protect forms. Site statistics use no cookies.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we publish the new version here and let you know in the dashboard.
